LK OBDII Scanner for Volvo

Privacy Policy Terms of Use Diagnostic Disclaimer

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LK OBDII Scanner for Volvo (the “App”) handles information when you use it. The App is an independent diagnostic application intended for compatible Volvo vehicles.

Important: This policy describes the current app design and the planned advertising integration separately. The developer should update this page before enabling AdMob or adding any other SDK or network feature.

1. Developer and contact

The App is developed and published by an individual developer.

  • Developer: LK
  • Email: lukaszkosz@o2.pl

For privacy questions or requests relating to processing for which the developer is responsible, contact the email address above.

2. No user account

The App does not require registration and does not create user accounts.

3. Vehicle and diagnostic information

The App may read vehicle information requested by you, such as VIN, ECU information, diagnostic trouble codes (DTCs), live parameters and other diagnostic readings.

Under the current design, this information is processed and stored locally on your device. The developer does not operate a server that receives this vehicle information, and the App is not designed to transmit it to the developer. Information stored locally remains until you delete it, clear the App's data or uninstall the App. Device backups may be subject to your Android and backup-provider settings.

4. Bluetooth and Android permissions

The App uses Bluetooth to communicate with a compatible ELM327 or other supported OBD-II adapter. Depending on your Android version and the functions used, Android may request Bluetooth, Nearby Devices or (on some older Android versions) Location permission for Bluetooth scanning.

These permissions are used for adapter discovery and communication. The App does not use Bluetooth scanning to track your physical location. Android permission labels and requirements may vary by OS version.

5. Internet connection

In the current version, the App does not upload vehicle diagnostic information to the developer or to a vehicle-data server. If an Internet connection is used by a future version for advertising or another third-party feature, the information handled by that service will be described in this policy before that feature is enabled.

6. Advertising and Google AdMob

The current release does not include advertising services, according to the developer's current description.

7. Analytics and other third-party services

According to the developer's current description, the App does not use a separate analytics service such as Firebase Analytics, cloud storage or an account system. If crash reporting, analytics, remote configuration, update checking, mediation or another third-party SDK is added, this policy and the Google Play Data safety information must be updated as appropriate.

8. Data retention and deletion

Vehicle and diagnostic information stored by the App locally remains on your device until you delete it, clear the App's storage or uninstall the App. The developer does not maintain a server-side copy of this information under the current design. Third-party advertising providers, once integrated, may apply their own retention and deletion policies.

9. Sharing and international transfers

The developer does not sell vehicle diagnostic information. Under the current design, locally stored vehicle data is not intentionally disclosed to third parties by the developer. If AdMob is enabled, advertising-related information may be transmitted directly from the device to Google and relevant advertising partners, which may process information in countries outside the European Economic Area under the safeguards described in their documentation.

10. Security

The developer aims to avoid unnecessary transmission of vehicle information. The security of locally stored information also depends on your device security, operating system, backups and access controls. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction or deletion of personal data, restriction of processing, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You may contact the developer at lukaszkosz@o2.pl regarding processing for which the developer is responsible.

For processing carried out independently by Google or another advertising provider, you may need to contact that provider directly. In Poland, you may lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO): uodo.gov.pl.

12. Children's privacy

The App is a vehicle diagnostic utility for a general audience and is not specifically designed for children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. The target-audience and advertising settings must be reviewed if the App's intended audience changes.

13. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the App's functionality, SDKs, data practices or legal requirements change. The current version and its effective date will be published on this page. Material changes should also be reflected in the App and relevant Google Play disclosures.
